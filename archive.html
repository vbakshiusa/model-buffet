Oct 2025 · 200K ctx · $1 / $5
Replaced by Haiku 5.5 (Oct 7, 2026)

Haiku 5.5 costs a tenth as much below 100K tokens (half the old rate above it), but its new tokenizer emits roughly 30% more tokens, so effective savings are closer to 87% below 100K and about 35% above.
