Sonnet 5.5 vs Sonnet 5

Same sticker price, very different model. Anthropic's pitch is a faster, cheaper complement to Opus 5.5 that's strongest at well-scoped everyday work — fixing bugs, and producing documents, slides, and spreadsheets. Most numbers below are Anthropic's own; the cost-per-task and speed rows come from Artificial Analysis testing.

What changedSonnet 5 · Jun 2026Sonnet 5.5 · Sept 28, 2026
API price / 1M tokens$2 in · $10 out$2 in · $10 out (unchanged)
Cost per taskBaseline20–41% cheaper at Low/Medium/High effort while scoring 12–15 points higher; ~5% cheaper at Xhigh
