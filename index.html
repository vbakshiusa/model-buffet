System One models

A different shape of model entirely — no prose in or out. You send text plus typed questions (a multiple choice, a 1–10 score, a yes/no), and it answers with calibrated probabilities in one parallel pass instead of generating tokens one at a time. Built for classification, routing, and guardrail decisions sitting in front of — or instead of — a chat model.
