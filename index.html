Growing

Decision models

Models that answer with a probability instead of writing text. You send text or an image plus typed questions (multiple choice, a 1–10 score, a yes/no) and get calibrated confidence back — built for classification, routing, and guardrail decisions in front of, or instead of, a chat model. Also marketed as “System One” models, TypeSafe’s term borrowed from Kahneman’s fast-versus-slow thinking. Two designs sit under the label: purpose-built models that answer every question in one parallel pass (Jev, Laya, Clef, Strands Decider, d1-ultra, Perplexity’s Decider), and OpenAI’s Decisions API, which constrains a regular chat model to a fixed list of answers. Skeptics call the purpose-built kind a zero-shot classifier with a new name — the task is old, and independent benchmarks are still thin. The shape of the output is guaranteed; the answers themselves are probabilistic.
